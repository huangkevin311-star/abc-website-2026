À propos

Qui sommes-nous ?

EDHEC Asian Business & Culture (ABC) est une association étudiante du campus de Lille de l’EDHEC Business School. Elle rassemble les étudiants autour d’un intérêt commun pour l’Asie, à la croisée de la culture et du business.

À travers nos événements, conférences et projets, nous faisons découvrir la diversité des cultures et traditions asiatiques tout en ouvrant des perspectives sur les enjeux économiques et les opportunités professionnelles en Asie.

Notre histoire

Créée en 2016, ABC célèbre cette année ses 10 ans. Depuis une décennie, dix générations d’étudiants se sont succédé, chacune apportant ses idées, ses projets et sa vision de l’association. Aujourd’hui, la 10e génération poursuit cette histoire et continue de faire évoluer ABC.

Concours finance · Nov 11, 2026

ABC Finance Consulting Case Competition 2026–2027

Think like an Investment Banker or a Strategy Consultant. Perform financial analysis. Advise a global company. Defend your strategy in front of a jury.

The ABC Finance Business Case Competition is the first case competition organised by Asian Business & Culture at EDHEC. It gives Bachelor students the chance to work on a real strategic question faced by a major Asian company, using the same tools as professionals in investment banking, private equity and consulting.

The case

This year's company

PDD Holdings

The Nasdaq-listed parent company of Temu, facing growing regulatory pressure in Europe.

Adapt, retreat, or rethink?

This year, teams advise PDD Holdings, the Nasdaq-listed parent company of Temu, on its response to growing regulatory pressure in Europe. Should it adapt, retreat, or rethink its model? Your team decides, and backs the recommendation with numbers.

AdaptRetreatRethink

Who can take part

The competition is open to 1st and 2nd year Bachelor students and 1st year PGE students from EDHEC, as well as eligible students from other universities in Europe and Asia, in teams of 3-5 people. No prior finance experience is required, only curiosity and the will to learn. Check where you stand.

Why take part

Prepare for the interviews that matter.

Market sizing, valuation and strategy questions come up in almost every investment banking, private equity and consulting interview. Here, you practise them on a real company before recruiters ask.

Learn the tools professionals use every day.

Comparable companies, DCF, precedent transactions, operating models: you'll build them yourself, not just read about them in class.

Gain experience you can actually talk about.

“I valued PDD Holdings and recommended its European strategy to a jury” is a far stronger interview answer than “I'm interested in finance.”

Get an EDHEC-certified certificate.

Every participant receives an official certificate from EDHEC Business School. Add it to your CV and LinkedIn as proof that you've completed a real, professional-level finance case.

Get noticed by experienced professionals.

Present your work to a jury of academics and professionals, and get feedback that sharpens how you think and present.

Build your network across two continents.

Meet ambitious students from EDHEC and partner universities in Europe and Asia.
